Welcome to Addis Ababa

Addis Ababa is the capital city of Ethiopia. It is a beautiful city with many historical places, cultural attractions and natural landscapes.

Discover Addis is a travel guide that helps visitors find interesting places to explore in Addis Ababa and nearby areas.

Featured Places

Entoto Mountain

Entoto is a popular place where visitors can enjoy nature and beautiful views of Addis Ababa.

National Museum of Ethiopia

The National Museum is a place where visitors can learn about Ethiopian history and culture.
